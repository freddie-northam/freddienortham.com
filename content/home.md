the tools brands use to reach their fans. I started working at 14 and never went to
university, so most of what I know came from shipping things that didn't work first.

This is where the writing lives, along with the side projects and the parts of my life
that have nothing to do with code. There are more of those than you might expect: a book
about classic cars has been in progress since 2023.
