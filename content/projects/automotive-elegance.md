A book, in progress since December 2023.

It covers the history, culture and passion surrounding classic and luxury cars, told
through the stories of individual cars and the people around them. The argument is that
these cars matter for reasons that have very little to do with engineering: they are
objects that carry status, memory, and a particular idea of beauty across generations.
